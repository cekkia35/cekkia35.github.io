Privacy Policy – İlan Ustası (Listing Master)

Last updated: 28 November 2026

This policy explains how the mobile game İlan Ustası (also shown as Listing Master, Inserat-Profi, Rey del Anuncio, Roi de l'Annonce, Re degli Annunci, Rei do Anúncio, Мастер Объявлений and 売買マスター; package name com.alkocyr.ilanusta), published on Google Play by Arcade Game&Retro Game - alkocyr ("we"), handles information. Contact: alkocyrgame@gmail.com.

1. Information we collect ourselves

We do not ask for your name, e-mail, phone number, location or contacts, and we do not run our own servers. Your game progress (money, vehicles, settings, language) is stored only on your device. All buyers, sellers, messages and vehicles in the game are fictional.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows banner ads between listings and rewarded ads only when you choose to watch one for an in-game bonus. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information and ad interaction data to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent with Google's consent tool (User Messaging Platform); you can change your choice at any time with the "Ad privacy settings" button in the game's News tab. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Buying the "Remove ads" pack turns off banner ads. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3. In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

Purchases (in-game money packs, remove ads, VIP and other boosts) are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details; we only receive a confirmation of which item was bought so it can be delivered and restored.

4. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

5. Data retention and deletion

Uninstalling the game, or using "Reset game" inside it, deletes the progress stored on your device. Data held by Google (ads, purchases) is kept and deleted under Google's policies. You can contact us at any time with questions or requests.

6. Security

All communication with Google services uses encrypted connections (HTTPS).

7. Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
