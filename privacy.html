Privacy Policy – Pixel Wings: Air Force

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This policy explains how the mobile game Pixel Wings: Air Force (package name com.RedStains.PixelWings), published on Google Play by Arcade Game&Retro Game - alkocyr ("we"), handles information. Contact: alkocyrgame@gmail.com.

1. Information we collect ourselves

We do not ask for your name, e-mail, phone number, location or contacts, and we do not run our own servers. Your game progress (coins, planes, upgrades, stars, settings) is stored on your device.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows rewarded ads only when you choose to watch one (for example to continue after losing or to double your coins). There are no forced ads. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information and ad interaction data to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent with Google's consent tool (User Messaging Platform); you can change your choice at any time from Settings > Ad privacy settings in the game. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3. In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

Purchases (coins, the VIP pack, the special plane) are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details; we only receive a confirmation of which item was bought so it can be delivered and restored.

4. Google Play Games Services

If you are signed in to Google Play Games, the game uses it for leaderboards and cloud save. Google may share your Play Games player profile (gamer name, avatar, player ID) with the game, your scores are posted to leaderboards, and a copy of your game progress is stored in your Google account so you do not lose it when you change devices. This is governed by the Google Privacy Policy; you can manage it in the Play Games app.

5. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

6. Data retention and deletion

Uninstalling the game deletes the progress stored on your device. To delete your cloud save, remove the game's data from your Play Games account settings, or contact us and we will help. Data held by Google (ads, purchases, Play Games) is kept and deleted under Google's policies.

7. Security

All communication with Google services uses encrypted connections (HTTPS).

8. Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
